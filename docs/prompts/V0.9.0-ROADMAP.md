### D1: Blessed Alternatives Research

**Current Issue:** blessed was abandoned in 2017, no security updates

**Alternatives to Evaluate:**

| Library | Pros | Cons | Migration Effort |
|---------|------|------|------------------|
| `ink` | React-based, active, modern | Different paradigm | High |
| `terminal-kit` | Active, feature-rich, well-documented | Less widget-focused | Medium |
| `neo-blessed` | Drop-in replacement, community fork | May inherit blessed issues | Low |
| `blessed-contrib` fork | Dashboard-specific widgets | Limited maintenance | Low |

**Evaluation Criteria:**
1. Active maintenance (commits in last 6 months)
2. Security vulnerability history
3. Widget support (tables, charts, forms)
4. Migration complexity from blessed
5. Performance with 50+ projects
