### D1: Blessed Alternatives Research ✅ COMPLETE

**Status:** ✅ Evaluation complete, Ink selected
**Date:** 2026-01-07
**Documents:**
- [TUI Library Evaluation](./TUI-LIBRARY-EVALUATION.md) - Full comparison matrix
- [POC Results](./POC-RESULTS.md) - Proof of concept findings

**Decision:** Migrate to **Ink** (React for CLIs)

**Rationale:**
1. **Most actively maintained** - Updated 16 days ago vs 9+ years for blessed
2. **Industry adoption** - Used by Terraform, Gatsby, Prisma, Shopify, NYT
3. **Code reduction** - 80-91% fewer lines (declarative vs imperative)
4. **Modern architecture** - React components, testable, composable

**Evaluation Results:**

| Library | Score | Status | Recommendation |
|---------|-------|--------|----------------|
| **Ink** | 85/100 🥇 | Active (Dec 2025) | ✅ **SELECTED** |
| terminal-kit | 79/100 🥈 | Active (Jan 2026) | ⚠️ Viable alternative |
| neo-blessed | 61/100 🥉 | Stale (May 2022) | ❌ Not recommended |
